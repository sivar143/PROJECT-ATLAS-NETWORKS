# ATLAS Enterprise Switch — Product Architecture

## Role

Enterprise access/aggregation switching platform.

## Required architecture areas

- high-density Ethernet
- 10/25GbE uplinks as appropriate
- redundant or field-serviceable power options where justified
- hardware-assisted ACLs
- VLAN/QinQ where required
- LACP
- STP family
- LLDP
- telemetry
- secure management
- centralized controller integration
- zero-touch provisioning

## Reliability

The enterprise family must support predictable thermal behavior, component lifecycle planning, manufacturing traceability and controlled software releases.

Exact port density and uplink mix are product-program decisions.
