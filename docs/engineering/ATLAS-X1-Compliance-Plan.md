# ATLAS X1 Compliance Engineering Plan

## 1. Objective

Design the product so certification can be completed without major PCB or enclosure redesign.

## 2. Compliance domains

Evaluate the markets selected for launch for:

- electrical safety
- EMC/EMI
- radio
- environmental substance restrictions
- material/environmental requirements
- power-adapter compliance
- labeling and documentation

## 3. Pre-compliance

Before formal certification:

- conducted emissions scan
- radiated emissions scan
- ESD
- EFT/surge as applicable
- RF conducted tests
- OTA tests
- temperature extremes within product specification
- abnormal operation
- adapter compatibility

## 4. RF

The final Wi-Fi 7 radio design must maintain required regulatory control over:

- allowed channels
- transmit power
- DFS where applicable
- 6 GHz operating modes
- antenna gain
- country configuration
- firmware regulatory database

## 5. Certification artifacts

Maintain:

- schematics
- PCB Gerbers
- BOM
- critical component list
- antenna specifications
- enclosure drawings
- RF block diagram
- software version
- test firmware
- user manual
- label artwork
- declarations and laboratory reports

## 6. Change control

Any post-certification change to RF components, antennas, clocks, shielding, power stages or enclosure geometry must trigger a compliance impact review.
